Smart Home: a control panel and mobile app for security, lighting and rooms

A wall-panel and mobile interface for a smart home: arm and disarm with a PIN, armed away and armed stay modes, fire alarm alerts, lighting with a color picker and room shortcuts.

Role
UI/UX designer
Users
Home owners
Platform
Wall panel (tablet) and mobile
Project
Freelance

At a glance

Problem

Make arming, disarming and alarms unmistakable and easy to use from the panel or a phone.

My role

As a freelance project I designed the UX and UI and the flows: the panel dashboard, the lighting controls, the alarm popups and the mobile safety flow.

Delivered

Panel screens, mobile screens and a flow diagram of the safety experience.

What I did

  • Designed PIN entry and a drag-to-arm control with Stay and Leaving options.
  • Designed armed away and armed stay states, with silent exit and entry delay options.
  • Designed fire alarm popups for the activated and normal states, with a confirmation step.
  • Designed lighting controls with brightness and a color picker.
  • Designed room shortcuts and a bottom navigation on the panel and a mobile safety flow.
