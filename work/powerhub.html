At a glance

Problem

Turn a large amount of site data (yield, inverters, meters, alarms) into screens operators can scan quickly.

My role

  • Designed alarm lists, a power chart view and operation reports.
  • Designed device management and alert settings with configurable thresholds.
  • Built the CMS UI in code.
