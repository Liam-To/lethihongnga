BeaHub: a booking and management platform for spas, salons and wellness businesses

A platform for multi-location spa, beauty and wellness businesses: online booking, staff and merchant management, billing, promotions and gift cards. I designed the product screens and the marketing material, and animated the boot logo.

Role
UI/UX and visual designer
Users
Business owners, staff and customers of spas and salons
Platform
Mobile apps, CMS and website
Focus
Product, marketing and motion

At a glance

Problem

Help multi-location spa, beauty and wellness businesses run bookings, staff and billing from one platform, and explain it clearly to new customers.

My role

I designed the mobile app and CMS screens, the pricing and login pages and the marketing material: Facebook ads, a tri-fold brochure, product videos and a boot logo animation.

Delivered

Mobile and CMS screens, pricing, login and support pages, five Facebook ads, a brochure, product videos and a boot logo animation.

What I did

  • Designed mobile screens for booking, including recurring appointments, checking an order, promotions and gift cards, an appointment list and a staff account.
  • Designed the CMS: a dashboard, merchant details with subscription plan, add-ons and devices, payment and invoices.
  • Designed the pricing page, the login screen and a support page.
  • Designed five Facebook ad visuals: a pricing offer, tracking bookings, turn allocation and earnings split, routing feedback, and digital gift cards.
  • Designed a tri-fold brochure.
  • Made the product videos on the BeaHub YouTube channel.
  • Created the boot logo animation.
