LinkedIn company banner for a print-on-demand partner (design assessment)

Two banner concepts for a global print-on-demand fulfillment partner, made as a design assessment for a Senior Digital Designer role.

Role
Designer
Type
Design assessment, 2026
Format
LinkedIn cover, 1128 x 191 px
Tools
Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator, AI-assisted imagery

At a glance

Problem

Make one company banner explain the company (a global, technology-driven print-on-demand fulfillment partner) to sellers, industry partners and first-time visitors in under three seconds.

My role

I designed two banner concepts for a design assessment, with the rationale, the safe zones for LinkedIn and the brand colors.

Delivered

Two concepts at 1128 x 191 px, exported at @1x and @2x, with the rationale and specs.

Design thinking

  • Audience: three viewers in one glance: eCommerce sellers, industry partners, and people who have never heard of the company.
  • Key message: a global, technology-driven print-on-demand fulfillment partner, said in one line and readable in under three seconds.
  • Two angles: Concept 01 explains the process (idea, product, doorstep). Concept 02 shows the scale (products and a global network).
  • Fresh eyes: I approached it as a newcomer to print-on-demand, the same position as a first-time visitor to the page, to keep the message simple and free of jargon.

Concept 01: Idea to Doorstep

An icon-led concept that tells the story as a process: one idea (the orange spark) moves through production, packing and global delivery. The connected icon system makes the supply chain easy to grasp, while the badge and the sub-line say what the company is.

Concept 02: Global Showcase

A product-led concept that shows scale and reach: printed products (apparel, drinkware, wall art and bags) in front of a connected world map. The AI-generated imagery was art-directed to feel authentic rather than template-like, and three pillars (On-Demand Production, Global Supply Chain, Tech-Driven Fulfillment) speak to sellers and partners.

Specs and usability

  • 1128 x 191 px, exported at @1x and @2x (PNG).
  • The bottom-left area of about 220 x 120 px is covered by the page logo on desktop and mobile, so it is kept as background only.
  • All copy starts at about x = 270 px and sits in the central zone, so it stays readable when LinkedIn scales the cover down on phones.
  • Brand colors, sampled from the company website: navy #012169, cyan #C5FAFF, orange #FF5C01.
