Fit4Stream: a live-event and community platform, from brand to front-end

A social networking and community platform that connects presenters and participants through posts, messaging, team discussions and live events.

Role
UI/UX designer and front-end developer
Users
Presenters, participants, community members
Platform
Web, mobile, CMS
Tools
Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop, Illustrator, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, Vue

At a glance

Problem

Create an engaging platform where presenters and participants can communicate, share and collaborate.

My role

I designed the brand, web, mobile and CMS experience, ran usability tests with customers, and built front-end screens.

Delivered

The Fit4Stream logo, web platform, mobile app, CMS, prototypes, front-end implementation, a marketing video and a brochure.

What I did

  • Designed the logo and visual identity.
  • Designed the web platform: information architecture, navigation, social interactions, posts, messaging and community features.
  • Designed the mobile app and the CMS interface.
  • Developed concepts, user flows, wireframes and high-fidelity UI from product and customer requirements.
  • Created interactive prototypes, and conducted usability testing with customers.
  • Translated approved designs into responsive HTML and CSS with Bootstrap and Vue to speed up development.
  • Created a marketing video and a brochure.
