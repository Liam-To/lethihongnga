About

Where design, engineering and complexity meet

I started in print and brand, moved to the web, and became a product designer as interfaces became systems.

Today I work on products where clarity matters: multi-theme displays for ships, security apps that need to be unmistakable, posture and health data turned into plain next steps, and design systems that engineers can build.

I design in Figma, build in HTML, SCSS, React, Vue and TypeScript, and add motion with Lottie and After Effects. Over 15 years I moved from graphic and print design through web and mobile to product design and design systems.

How I lead

As Head Creative Designer at Renaissance School (2013 to 2016) I led creative and UI/UX design across web, mobile and digital products, set design direction, introduced responsive design practices, planned projects, assigned tasks and coordinated with sales, management and development. I also managed design quality, project decisions and delivery risk.

As Lead Designer at Cateno, a software company in Norway (2011 to 2013), I led UI/UX and visual design for web and mobile projects, coordinated with sales and management, managed project risk, and introduced emerging design practices to the team.

How I work with AI

I use Claude, Figma AI and Magnific in daily work for ideation, UI exploration, visual assets and wireframe references. I created custom 3D characters and environments for a children's app with AI-assisted workflows. I review AI output before it goes into a design.
